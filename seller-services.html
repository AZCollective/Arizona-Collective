Our Listing Strategy

How we get you
top dollar.

01
Strategic Pricing

Pricing is the single most important decision in your sale. We run a thorough CMA — not just comps, but active competition, buyer demand, and market momentum — to land on a number that attracts offers fast.

02
Professional Presentation

Every listing gets professional photography, a 3D walkthrough, and a custom marketing package. First impressions happen online — we make sure yours stops scrolling.

03
Maximum Exposure

Your home hits Zillow, Realtor.com, and 50+ platforms the day it lists. We also market directly to our buyer network and active agents — generating interest before your first showing.

04
Expert Negotiation

Multiple offers are a position of power — but only if you know how to use them. We evaluate every offer on price, terms, and risk, then negotiate to maximize your net proceeds.

05
Transaction Management

From accepted offer to close, we manage every inspection, appraisal, and escrow milestone so nothing falls through the cracks and your timeline stays on track.

06
Relocation Coordination

Selling and buying simultaneously? We coordinate both sides of your move — timing your close, finding your next home, and managing it all as one seamless transaction.
