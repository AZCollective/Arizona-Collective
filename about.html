Founding Partner

Derrick "Rick" Thram

Licensed Realtor · Keller Williams Integrity First Realty

Rick Thram is a full-time licensed Realtor with Keller Williams Integrity First Realty in Gilbert, Arizona, with 34 years of experience serving home buyers, sellers, and investors. Over the course of his career, Rick has successfully closed more than 900 real estate transactions, representing over $250 million in total sales volume. His disciplined pricing and negotiation strategies have resulted in a 99.7% average list-to-sale price.

Rick is also a licensed Realtor in Minnesota where he serves as a Senior Partner of The Norwood Group of Edina Realty, a highly experienced team with a combined 60 years of real estate sales experience. In this leadership role, Rick helps guide strategy, pricing, and client representation across a wide range of residential and commercial transactions.

Rick is focused on serving in residential real estate sales in Scottsdale, Gilbert, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, and Queen Creek, Arizona. He also brings 14 years of commercial real estate experience, having sold dozens of commercial properties and businesses. His combined residential, commercial, and leadership background provides clients with informed guidance, steady leadership, and trusted results.
